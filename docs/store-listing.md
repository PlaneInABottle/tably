## Short description

Create or join nearby plans for today or tomorrow, confirm your seat, and coordinate in a focused group chat.

## Full description

Tably turns "we should do something" into a real small-group plan.

Discover nearby tables for coffee, dinner, walks, runs, cinema, coworking, culture, sports, and exploring. Join a table, answer a short intro, confirm attendance, and use guided chat prompts to settle the details.

Table momentum keeps plans honest: held seats expire when people do not respond, waitlisted members can be promoted, and creators can cancel plans they cannot host. Built-in reporting and blocking help users manage unsafe or unwanted interactions.

Foreground location is optional and used only when you choose your current position for a table. Notifications are optional and can be enabled and managed from Settings.
