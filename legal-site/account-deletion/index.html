Available directly in the app

Delete in Tably

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Review both confirmation prompts and confirm permanent deletion.

Deletion signs you out and removes your account, profile, owned tables, table membership, messages, push tokens, and stored profile photo through the service's deletion process.

Request help

If you cannot access the app, email support@tably.app from the address connected to your account. We may ask you to verify ownership.

Records that may remain

Limited safety, fraud-prevention, transaction, or legal records may be retained where reasonably necessary or required by law. Retained records are not used to keep your public profile active.
