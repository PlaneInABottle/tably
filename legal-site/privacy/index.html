Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

This policy explains how Tably processes information when you use the mobile application and related services.

Information we process

  • Account information, including email address, display name, profile text, and optional profile photo.
  • Table content, including plan details, approximate map coordinates, membership status, intro answers, confirmations, and table messages.
  • Safety information, including reports, blocks, and snapshots needed to review reported content.
  • Device push tokens and notification preferences when you enable notifications.
  • Technical information needed for authentication, security, diagnostics, and service operation.

Location

Tably requests foreground location only when you choose to use your current location. Table coordinates are used for nearby discovery. Do not place a table at a private home or another sensitive location.

How information is used

We use information to operate accounts, discover and coordinate tables, send requested notifications, prevent abuse, investigate reports, maintain security, and improve reliability.

Sharing

Profile and table information is shared with other signed-in users as required by the product. We use service providers for hosting, authentication, storage, and notifications. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed for security, legal obligations, and abuse prevention. You can delete your account in Settings or follow the account deletion instructions. Safety records may be retained where necessary to protect users or comply with law.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, disable notification categories, revoke device permissions, block users, leave tables, and delete your account.

Contact

Email privacy questions to support@tably.app.
