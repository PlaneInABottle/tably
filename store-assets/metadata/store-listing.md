### Description

Tably helps people meet around a specific plan instead of an endless group chat.

Explore nearby tables on a map or list, filter by activity, and see the time, area, vibe, capacity, and confirmation status before joining. Hosts can create structured plans for coffee, dinner, drinks, walks, runs, cinema, coworking, culture, sports, and more.

Once a table forms, Tably keeps the practical details in one place. Confirm attendance, review the meeting point, see who is joining, and use guided chat prompts for venue details, arrival time, or last-minute updates.

Key features:

- Map-first discovery with activity filters
- Clear table status, capacity, and confirmation details
- Structured table creation with a precise meeting point
- Upcoming plans and attendance state in one view
- Guided group chat for practical coordination
- Profile, notification, safety, and account controls

Tably is designed for real plans with a clear purpose, place, and time.
